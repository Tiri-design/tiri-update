Privacy and contact

About this page

This page updates Tiri hub prototypes that are being tested. Tiri is based in Taiwan. For questions, problems or a security concern, write to us at hello@tiri-launchpad.com.

Privacy

  • This page uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and loads nothing from other websites.
  • When you connect your hub, your browser talks to it over USB. It reads the hub's version, sets the hub's clock to your computer's time and writes the update. All of this stays between your browser and the hub. Nothing is sent to us.
  • The page is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address. You'll find the details in GitHub's General Privacy Statement on docs.github.com.
  • If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed. You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at the address above.

What an update changes

An update replaces the hub's software and nothing else. Your tags, their names, the weekly plan and all settings stay as they are, because the page never writes to the part of the hub where they're kept. Every piece of the update is checked against its published checksum before and after it's written.

Open-source software

The hub's software and this page are built with open-source software. See the licences and source.
