Privacy and contact

About this page

This page updates Tiri hub prototypes that are being tested. Tiri is based in Taiwan. Questions, problems or a security concern: hello@tiri-launchpad.com.

Privacy

  • This page uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and loads nothing from other websites.
  • When you connect the hub, your browser talks to it over USB: it reads the hub's version and writes the update. That stays between your browser and the hub; nothing is sent to us.
  • The page is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address; see GitHub's General Privacy Statement on docs.github.com.
  • If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed. You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at the address above.

What an update changes

An update replaces the hub's firmware only. Tag names, settings, the weekly plan and the paired tags stay: the page never writes the hub's settings area. Every part is checked against its published checksum before and after writing.
